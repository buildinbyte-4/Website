About The University

Sanskrit, Science, and Modern Technology in Harmony

Established under the benign blessings of Their Holinesses Pujyasri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetam Sankaracharyas, SCSVMV was established with the vision of integrating ancient heritage with modern scientific temper.

Situated in the historic temple town of Kanchipuram, our campus offers world-class educational infrastructure alongside rich cultural values and multidisciplinary research opportunities.

Interdisciplinary Research Culture

SCSVMV encourages active collaboration between traditional knowledge archives and modern scientific research. Our dedicated incubation center supports faculty and student startups.

🔬 Center for Indian Knowledge Systems: Digitization and scientific analysis of ancient manuscripts.
⚡ Center for Energy & Smart Grid: Research in renewable solar technologies and smart power management.
🤖 Robotics & Automation Lab: Hands-on prototyping and embedded software innovation.
