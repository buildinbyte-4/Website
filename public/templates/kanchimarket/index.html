About KSM

Heritage, Elegance & Modern Freshness

Located in the historic heart of Kanchipuram, KSM (Kanchi Super Market) combines traditional hospitality with an ultra-modern luxury grocery and gourmet dining estate.

From organic harvest directly sourced from surrounding boutique farms to world-class suite accommodations, KSM redefines the modern lifestyle experience.
