# Client Case Study Plan

Status: planning document only. Nothing in this file is approved for publication. Every client name, quotation, image, result, and metric must receive written client approval before it is added to the website.

## Purpose

Turn verified client engagements into credible case studies that explain the business problem, BuildInByte's work, and the measurable outcome. The final pages should help prospective clients understand both the engineering approach and the commercial value without exposing confidential information.

## Information to collect from each client

- Approved client name, industry, location, and logo usage.
- The original business problem and who experienced it.
- Previous workflow, tools, constraints, and known risks.
- Project scope, delivery period, team involvement, and launch date.
- Features and integrations that may be discussed publicly.
- Approved technology details and any security-sensitive exclusions.
- Before-and-after measures, their source, and the measurement period.
- A named testimonial, job title, and explicit permission to publish it.
- Approved screenshots, photography, brand assets, and anonymization requirements.
- Links or calls to action the client permits BuildInByte to publish.

## Measurable-results checklist

Use only figures that the client can substantiate. Record the baseline, final value, date range, sample size, and owner for each metric.

- Time saved per task, week, or reporting cycle.
- Reduction in manual steps, errors, support requests, or operating cost.
- Improvement in conversion, response time, throughput, uptime, or customer satisfaction.
- Adoption figures such as active users, teams onboarded, or transactions processed.
- Qualitative outcomes when quantitative data is unavailable, clearly labeled as client observations.

## Proposed page structure

1. Hero: approved client identity, one-sentence outcome, industry, service, and delivery period.
2. Challenge: the original workflow, affected users, constraints, and stakes.
3. Solution: product strategy, key capabilities, and why the chosen approach fit.
4. Implementation: phases, collaboration model, integrations, and notable engineering decisions.
5. Outcome: verified metrics, operational improvements, and client-approved evidence.
6. Technology: concise architecture and stack details that are safe to disclose.
7. Testimonial: approved quotation with name, role, and organization.
8. Call to action: a relevant consultation prompt rather than a generic sales message.

## Image requirements

- One 16:9 hero image at a minimum of 1600×900.
- Three to six interface or workflow images with captions explaining their relevance.
- Client logo supplied or explicitly approved by the client.
- Remove personal data, credentials, internal URLs, financial details, and confidential records.
- Capture consistent desktop and mobile views where both experiences are relevant.
- Confirm that BuildInByte has publication rights for every supplied asset.

## Approval workflow

1. Internal owner completes the questionnaire and evidence sheet.
2. BuildInByte drafts the case study with all unverified material marked as placeholders.
3. Technical owner verifies architecture and security-sensitive content.
4. Client verifies facts, metrics, quotation, identity, and imagery in writing.
5. BuildInByte records the approval date and approved version.
6. Only the approved version is implemented and published.
7. Reconfirm permission before making material edits or adding new performance claims.
